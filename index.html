Project Timeline

5th Semester
Minor Project Foundation

Initiated the Helmet Detection minor project. Explored and implemented YOLOv11 to successfully detect helmets on riders from higher vantage points.

6th Semester
First Website & Expansion

Developed the first complete Helmet Detection dashboard. Integrated three core modules: Helmet Detection, Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR), and OCR.

7th Semester
Vehicle Speed Estimation

Expanded our computer vision capabilities by creating a second comprehensive dashboard focusing on real-time vehicle speed estimation and counting.

8th Semester
Tripling & Compliance Monitor

Finalized the suite with the Road Sentinel / Tripling Detection repository, creating a complete ecosystem for traffic violation monitoring.
